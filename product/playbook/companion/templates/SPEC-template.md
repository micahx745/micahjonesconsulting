# SPEC.md — the one page the AI keeps re-reading

<!-- Six sections. One page, hard cap. If it spills, NOT and LATER
     take the excess. Read at the start of every session. -->

# WHAT
One sentence: the product, who it's for, and the behavior that means
it's winning.

# NOT
What this product refuses to be or do. The anti-scope. Every "while
we're at it, should I add..." gets answered here, as a lookup.

# SHAPE
The architecture in six lines or fewer: where data lives, who is
allowed to see what, what talks to what.

# RULES
Your top invariants, or a pointer to the invariants file.

# NOW
The current milestone, one line, with what "done" means for it.

# LATER
The parking lot. Ideas wait here instead of leaking into scope.
