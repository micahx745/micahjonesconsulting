# Pre-flight - The paperwork pass (chapter 7)

- [ ] Answer the three questions in writing: health data for providers? EU users? enterprise questionnaires?

- [ ] Write the honest privacy policy: what you collect, why, how long, how deletion works.
- [ ] Make deletion a query, and prove it on a test account.
- [ ] Hold the SOC 2 line: a one-page security summary until a real contract demands the audit.
