# Prompt — draft outreach around MY specifics (chapter 8)

Draft a short outreach email using the four-part anatomy: context
that is really about them, proof I looked, one concrete work-shaped
offer, one one-step ask.

The specifics below are mine and must appear unchanged; write the
connective tissue around them, in plain language, no hype:

- Who they are: <name, what they do>
- The mutual context: <who introduced us / what of theirs I read>
- Their own words I'm quoting: "<exact sentence>"
- The work-shaped offer: <what I will DO for them>
- The one-step ask: <reply / pick a time / send the file>

Keep it under 150 words. If any sentence could be sent to anyone,
rewrite it or cut it.
