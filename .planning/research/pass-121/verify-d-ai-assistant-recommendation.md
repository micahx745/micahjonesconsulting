## Contrary evidence found

- **C12 contradicted by a larger, more current study.** wellows.com's 22.7M-citation, 1.15M-question dataset (Jan–Jun 2026) — larger and more recent than the sources that produced the brief's 11–12%/7.9/21.9 figures — finds only 6.1–6.5% website-level overlap between engines and much closer per-answer source counts (ChatGPT 4.2 vs Perplexity 4.7, not 7.9 vs 21.9). Direction unchanged (engines diverge), magnitude overstated by roughly 2x on overlap and understated the gap-narrowing on per-answer counts.
- **C13's headline figures do not appear in either source cited for them.** sanbi.ai's real, disclosed numbers (Reddit 2.0% of Perplexity's citations, Claude brand-domain share 14.7%, Claude Reddit/YouTube share <0.1%) are an order of magnitude different from "40.1% overall... zero on Claude... brand sites take 64%." This is the strongest single contrary finding in this review, because the brief calls this claim its most encouraging, most actionable data point.
- **C8's currency is overstated by about 14 months.** The Vercel source backing the "no JS rendering, 2026 log studies" claim is dated December 2024, not 2026.
- Vendor/marketing blogs (ziptie.ai, sanbi.ai, 1digitalagency.com, seresa.io, runmarshal.com, the-seo-autopilot.com) repeatedly recycle the same handful of underlying stats (11% overlap, 4.4x conversion, Reddit dominance) with drifting numbers and inconsistent methodology disclosure between write-ups — exactly the pattern the brief's own "Now overused" section warns about, and it caught two of its own citations in that pattern (C12, C13).

## Summary (5 lines)

The mechanical/crawler-policy claims (C1, C2, C4–C7) and the well-disclosed, single-vendor-with-real-methodology studies (Ahrefs: C10, C11, C14; the arXiv papers: C15, C16; Google's FAQ deprecation: C17) all check out and can be used as written. The two claims the brief itself flags as most consequential and most encouraging for the owner — C12 (cross-engine source overlap) and especially C13 (Claude favoring brand sites over Reddit) — do not survive a source check: their cited URLs either don't contain the stated numbers or, in wellows' case, a newer larger study contradicts them by roughly 2x. C3, C8, C9, C18, and C19 are directionally real but each rests on either a stale citation, a vendor with a commercial stake, or a citation that doesn't match its own content when fetched. Net effect for the site: keep the mechanical recommendations (robots.txt allow-listing, visible prose over hidden schema, real update dates) at full confidence; drop the specific "40.1%/zero-on-Claude/64%" number from any internal or client-facing framing and replace it with the weaker, source-confirmed claim that Claude showed a near-zero (not literally zero) Reddit/YouTube citation rate in one undisclosed-category study. Treat "off-site presence matters more than on-page work" (C20) as a reasonable hypothesis, not a proven synthesis, since two of its three supporting legs were refuted as cited.
