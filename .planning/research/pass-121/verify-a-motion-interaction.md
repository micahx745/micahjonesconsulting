## Contrary evidence found

- **C3/C20 (cross-document View Transitions):** caniuse's dedicated page (`caniuse.com/cross-document-view-transitions`, checked 2026-09-16) shows Safari 18.2+ with full support and 87.78% global coverage — directly contradicts the facet's "Chromium-only... Safari and Firefox still do not support them" framing. Firefox is the actual remaining gap (partial support behind a flag through v159).

The primary w3techs source (fetched directly) contradicts this and matches the facet instead; noted here so the discrepancy isn't silently dropped.
- **C16 attribution:** the facet's cited URL for the Gary Illyes quote does not itself name Illyes; a separate search confirms the underlying fact independently (Illyes at Google Search Central Deep Dive, July 2025), but the citation-to-claim mapping in the facet is loose.
- **C17 sourcing gap:** the facet flagged its own quotations/statistics/citations lift figures as needing primary-source verification. That primary source exists and is findable (Aggarwal et al., "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization," KDD 2024, arXiv:2311.09735) — the facet under-researched a claim it could have grounded more solidly, while over-trusting secondary blog aggregation for the adjacent 75x/80% figures in the same claim.

## Summary (what survives)

The hard browser-support and library-economics claims (C1, C2, C4, C5, C6, C11, C16) hold up well under direct re-fetch and are safe to brief from. The Web Almanac performance/accessibility statistics (C7–C9) are directionally credible but rest on one un-vetted secondary aggregator, not a primary-source check — usable with that caveat stated, not as settled measurement. The single most consequential error is C3/C20: Safari has supported cross-document View Transitions since v18.2, so the "Chromium-only" risk framing that talks the brief out of a cross-document thumbnail-to-hero morph is wrong — only Firefox is actually excluded, and that changes the honest cost/benefit for item 4 in "What this site could take." The live-examples table needs three corrections before it goes in a brief: Oryzo is satire and misnamed by award tier, Uncommon Studio's domain has moved, and Mat Voyce's URL would not resolve in this session.
