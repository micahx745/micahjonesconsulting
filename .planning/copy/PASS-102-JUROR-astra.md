A23 / `1rurcqf` explicitly names distribution; A24 / `1tzrp1h` names payments and repeatable traffic. At 390px, the first large noun has to earn the next few seconds. An unfamiliar service category creates work for the reader.

**For keeping it:** the site sells a broader combination than distribution. A26 / `1r5pb34` concerns what to build and cut; A30 / `1qgapzg` concerns whether to build or sell first; A27 / `1psez8u` concerns maintaining a product. The existing body already translates the category into the gap between a build and what buyers pay for.

**My call: keep “I build the go-to-market.” against the submitted alternative.** The headline can name the service while the body makes its relevance plain. T1’s “distribution” changes the service being named, and its accompanying “after the build” sentence makes that narrowing stronger. The vocabulary mismatch deserves attention; this replacement does not solve it faithfully.
