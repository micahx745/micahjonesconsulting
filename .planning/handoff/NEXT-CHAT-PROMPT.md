# Prompt to open the next chat

Paste the block below into a fresh session in this repo. Everything it needs is
on disk except the Reddit package, which the operator supplies.

---

New phase on the consulting site: act on the research, not on instinct.

Boot: read .claude/RESUME.md, then .planning/handoff/PHASE-RESEARCH-ACTION.md.
That brief names the artifacts, the open decisions and the traps. Read it before
proposing anything.

The three research packages:
  .planning/research/02-BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md   my offers, my verbatim copy, my
                                              claims and their provenance, and
                                              what evidence I do NOT have
  .planning/reviews/PERSONA-REVIEW-2026-09-03.md   six personas against the live
                                              site, scored, already acted on
  the Reddit evidence package                 5,456 posts, ten subreddits, a year
                                              measured bottom-up

THE REDDIT PACKAGE IS NOT IN THE REPO YET. I will paste or drop it in. Save it to
.planning/research/01-REDDIT-EVIDENCE.md before you use it - do not work from a
summary in chat. Same for any other research I hand you: on disk first.

The work: the playbook and the website, changed on what the research says.
Wording, services, the book, design, SEO.

First move - do NOT start editing. Read all three packages, then produce ONE
document: a research-to-action map that lists, per finding, (a) the market
evidence, (b) my current copy verbatim, (c) the proposed change, (d) whether it
needs a ruling from me. I rule on that map before any page changes.

Hard rules, all detailed in the brief: never publish a fact the claims ledger
does not carry; quote my copy verbatim rather than paraphrasing it; the industry
author is never named; a review is a reader, not an oracle - verify against the
live DOM before acting.

---

## Why the prompt is shaped this way

- **It forbids editing first.** The failure mode of this phase is an
  instinct-led pass wearing a research label. The research-to-action map makes
  the evidence visible before any page moves.
- **It names the missing package loudly.** `reddit-seed.md` in the repo is the
  crawl SEED, not findings. A session that confuses the two will produce
  market-informed-sounding advice with no market behind it.
- **It says "on disk first".** The six-persona review existed only in a chat
  transcript and had to be rescued at session boundary. Do not repeat that.
- **It repeats the four hard rules** rather than trusting the brief to be read
  carefully, because these are the ones whose breach ships something wrong to a
  live site.
