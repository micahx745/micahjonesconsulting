Motion

One gesture, and a still page that is designed rather than tolerated.

The signature: per-character decode

Locomotive splits its text one span per glyph so each character animates on its own. The headline above is doing it right now — reload to watch it again.

It needs no scroll, costs almost nothing, behaves identically at 390px, and the background composition drifts on its own. That is the whole motion budget.

What you see, with animations off

Your machines run MinAnimate = 0, so this page has already skipped every animation for you — the headline was solid on arrival and the background is static.

That is the point. The still page is the design, not the fallback. If it only works moving, it does not work.
