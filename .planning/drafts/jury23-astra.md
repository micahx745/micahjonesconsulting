Four fixes:

- “That sentence is the problem, not the layout.” → “Unclear messaging is one possible cause; first check traffic relevance, mobile usability, and whether signup works.”
- “If three of the five describe the problem you meant, the copy holds.” → “Use their answers to spot misunderstandings; five people cannot establish that the copy converts.”

**POST 3 — KILLS IT? Almost.** The outreach sequence is usable, but several confident rules could push a builder into unpaid work and unnecessary features.

Three fixes:

- “If the reply is a no, ask what they did the last time this came up.” → “Respect a clear no; ask a discovery question only if they invite further conversation.”
- “If they ask for a discount, ask which part of the result did not happen.” → “Ask what is driving the request: budget, uncertainty about value, or purchasing constraints.”
- “If they say they need to think about it, ask what they would need to see to decide, build that one thing, and send it.” → “Clarify what blocks the decision; agree on scope and a purchase commitment before building anything custom.”

**Founder risk:** Disclose pricing before guided setup. Treat funnel gaps as hypotheses: calls without setups do not prove you mishandled the demo.

**Search/citation improvement:** Add **“When should I mention pricing?”** Answer directly: before the prospect commits time to a trial or setup.

**Both:** Remove the promotional credential footer and service/work links. They conflict with the operator’s explicit no-pitch brief.
