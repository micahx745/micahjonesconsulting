## Direction 1 — The Working Session

*A held editorial frame lets each commitment take over the room in sequence, like four moments in one working relationship.*

A buyer experiences Scope, Plan, Build, and Stay one at a time—not as interchangeable services. The persistent heading establishes the method; changing scale and position show momentum from diagnosis to independence. The reference move is unseen.co’s type-led pacing, tempered by by-kin.com’s restraint.

At 1440, use a 420vh espresso section containing a 100vh sticky frame. “How I work.” sits at top left in 18px/500 Bricolage—not poster-sized—leaving the active commitment dominant. Each step occupies the lower two-thirds: the mono label at 13px, headline at `clamp(56px, 5.8vw, 84px)`, weight 520, and body at 19px/1.45 in a 500px column. The compositions evolve: Scope begins left; Plan moves its body to the center column; Build spans widest, with its body on the right; Stay returns headline and body into one resolved left alignment. Inactive steps remain as four quiet labels along the bottom edge, never numbered. Copper appears only as a 2px baseline that advances beneath those labels. “See the work” follows Stay.

At 390, abandon sticky behavior: four naturally sized acts stack with 72px between them. Headline size is `clamp(36px, 11vw, 46px)`, body 17px, with alternating 0 and 24px indents. Every body remains visible; the copper baseline becomes a short rule above each label.

On desktop, native scrolling changes the active composition over roughly 75vh per step. Headlines rise 28px and sharpen from weight 300 to 560 over 700ms; bodies follow after 120ms. Nothing loops and scrolling is untouched. Reduced motion, unsupported browsers, and no-JS receive the complete stacked composition. The outer 420vh track and fixed-size sticky frame reserve all space, while transforms and opacity prevent CLS.

/services uses bone, ink type, and copper-deep for the baseline and link. It keeps the editorial repositioning but uses a shorter 320vh track so the lighter page feels brisker.

The rejection risk is that it resembles a presentation carousel. Native scroll, persistent neighboring labels, and the complete mobile/static layout keep it recognizably part of the page.

## Direction 2 — The Marked-Up Proof

*All four commitments remain visible as one irregular editorial page, with changing margins making the work feel progressively more concrete.*

The buyer can scan the entire method immediately, then follow its stepped reading path from upper left to lower right. Each commitment gains a distinct spatial role without becoming a card. The borrowed move is uncommonstudio.com.au’s disciplined grid breaking, with linear.app’s print-like rules.

At 1440, place the section on ink with a 12-column grid and 140px vertical padding. “How I work.” spans columns 1–5 at 72px/0.9, weight 650. Scope begins beside it in columns 7–12; Plan drops below across columns 2–8; Build pushes beyond the normal text measure across columns 4–12; Stay closes in columns 1–9, with “See the work” aligned to its final body line. Headlines use `clamp(42px, 4.4vw, 64px)`, weight 480; bodies are 18px/1.5 and 440–520px wide. Labels are 12px mono. Vertical gaps deliberately vary—72px, 128px, then 88px—so the page reads as edited, not generated. Fine copper rules extend from each label only as far as its headline begins; there is no connecting route or diagram.

At 390, the grid becomes four columns. Heading is 50px; Scope and Stay align left, Plan indents 20px, and Build indents 40px before returning its body to 20px. Headlines are 34–42px according to length, preserving hierarchy without forcing theatrical wrapping. Gaps remain unequal at 56px, 88px, and 64px.

As each block enters the middle 65% of the viewport, its copper rule draws over 650ms and the headline’s letter spacing settles from `0.025em` to `-0.025em`; the body fades upward 12px over 520ms. The section never holds. Reduced motion and no-JS show the final poster composition; reserved grid tracks, fixed margins, and transform-only motion keep CLS effectively zero.

/services flips the ground to cream, uses ink throughout, and changes copper to copper-deep. The same broken grid is retained, but the heading occupies less height to suit the denser service page.

The rejection risk is decorative zigzagging. The direction avoids it by deriving every offset from the 12-column grid and limiting variation to alignment, spacing, type, and rules.
