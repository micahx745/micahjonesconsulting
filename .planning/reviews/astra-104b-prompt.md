# Juror look: the rebuilt home, Pass 104b

You are looking at one page at two widths. The two images are the same home page:
one at 390 wide (phone) and one at 1440 wide (desktop). Both are full-page captures
taken after fonts loaded and after every entrance animation had settled, so what you
see is the finished frame, not a mid-animation state.

The page sells consulting work: an audit at 2,500 dollars, packages from 500 dollars,
and a book. The buyer is a solo builder or a small team who used AI coding tools to
build something that demos well and then stalled before production. They are deciding
whether this person is worth 2,500 dollars of their money.

Answer these five questions and nothing else.

1. THE THIRTY SECOND READ. At each width separately, what does a buyer actually take
   away in the first thirty seconds? What does this person do, who is it for, and why
   them and not someone cheaper? Answer for 390 and for 1440 as two separate reads,
   because they scroll differently. If the two widths tell different stories, say so.

2. THE HERO SIGN. The top of the page pairs a headline with a video of a hand. Does
   that read as PROOF that this person builds real things, or as DECORATION that any
   agency site could have bought? Say which, and say what in the image made you decide.

3. THE OPERATOR BAND. Further down there is a wide band with film of a workspace in
   it. It exists to answer a specific complaint that the earlier version of this page
   got: "the video is too big and pixelated." Judge only whether the band now looks
   deliberate and sharp at the size it occupies, or whether it still reads as an
   oversized, soft image someone dropped in.

4. THE PACKAGES BAND. The pricing section sits on a dark full-bleed ground with three
   cards and a fourth block under them. It was rebuilt to be the most spectacular
   thing on the page, because it is where the money is. Is it? If something else on
   the page is louder, name that thing.

5. THREE CHANGES, RANKED. The three changes you would make, most valuable first. For
   each: what to change, and the one sentence of reasoning. Be concrete and specific
   to what you can see.

SCOPE. Do not re-litigate settled decisions. The following are the operator's own
rulings and are NOT open: the dark full-bleed ground under the packages section; the
absence of any client logo wall, testimonial or trust bar; the two-typeface system
with no monospace; the single copper accent; and the use of type and real footage
only, with no stock photography, illustration or icon kits. Judge what is there.

Write plainly. No preamble, no summary of these instructions. Answer 1 through 5 in
order under those headings.
