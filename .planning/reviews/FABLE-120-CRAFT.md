## Verdict

The four drafts are one revision from shippable, and none needs a rebuild. The record now
reads as one person with one method, because the operator settled the three things my record
ruling left open: the method is a connective line on /work, Postmates and Neuton are rows, and
$14M is "sourced and closed." The drafts apply most of the 2026-09-15 rulings and miss three:
the RFP study still cuts the positioning half that /services already states as fact, the
content engine's title claims a monthly rate the operator refused, and the birth worker's
close is the Guardicore close with words removed, pointed at an enterprise pain line. Two
closes share a beat and two share a sentence. The rest is line editing: four titles or
descriptions over the cap, one ledger word ("playbook"), one ledger phrase ("into the accounts
that carried the IPO"), and a handful of passives.

What blocks ship is not in the drafts. It is the live site. Fourteen surfaces still say the
two anonymous clients are one person, still carry 290,000 and 36x, still put tenure years
beside his roles, still count fourteen practitioners inside Ordani, and still say "one of four
companies" inside two studies. The moment any draft ships without that sweep, the site
contradicts itself on the same page. The sweep table below is the first item, and it ships in
the same commit as the studies or before them.
