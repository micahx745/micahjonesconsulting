VERDICT: SHOW HIM AFTER FIXES | Astra: credible build-and-sell range, but the strongest evidence arrives late and several sentences sound more constructed than convincing.

1. /full-time, opening at 1440 and 390: The seat is clear. A founder has reason to keep reading, but the desktop opening spends nearly an entire viewport introducing the argument without showing a concrete example. At 390, the gaps around “How I think” also delay the evidence. Move CODE first in the principle list and reduce the space around that section heading. Establish the builder immediately; the sales evidence can follow.

2. RESULT headline: “I build software against a result that can be counted.”
“Against a result” is unnatural, and “can be counted” withholds the useful distinction: this software helped win business. Yes, this reads as a slogan.
Rewrite: “I build software that helps close deals.”

The supporting sentences also need tightening:
“For an award-winning author and leadership consultant, I was the sole builder of an AI RFP engine.”
The client description delays the important fact: sole builder.
Rewrite: “I was the sole builder of an AI RFP engine for an award-winning author and leadership consultant.”

“The result: $3M in signed contracts, and a close rate from one in eight to one in four inside six months.”
The rate needs a verb and a denominator.
Rewrite: “Within six months, signed contracts totaled $3M and the close rate rose from one in eight proposals to one in four.”

3. /about, CURRENTLY at both widths: “I am taking new engagements now.” followed immediately by “I want one full-time seat: the first person at an early-stage company who both builds the product and sells it.”
This is honest range, presented as two competing asks. The abrupt transition makes the reader supply the relationship.
Rewrite the second sentence: “I’m also looking for one full-time role: an early-stage company’s first person to build the product and sell it.”
Give it visible paragraph separation. This explains the additional interest without inventing a priority, availability commitment or ORDANI plan.

4. POSITIONING body: “At Guardicore the pitch led with honeypots, but in deals, customer interviews and data analysis I found that buyers could not see the east-west traffic between their own workloads.”
The discovery is strong; the crowded middle makes it harder to follow.
Rewrite: “Guardicore’s pitch led with honeypots. Through deals, customer interviews and data analysis, I found that buyers could not see east-west traffic between their workloads.”

“I moved the story to east-west visibility and sat in the deals: $14M in revenue, sourced and closed, at a $1.2M average enterprise deal.”
“Sat in the deals” sounds like observation and weakens the ownership established by “sourced and closed.”
Rewrite: “I shifted the pitch to east-west visibility. I sourced and closed $14M in revenue, with an average enterprise deal of $1.2M.”

5. SCOPE headline: “I narrow the promise to what can be priced, policed and sold.”
The business judgment is relevant, but the alliteration calls attention to the writing, and “policed” takes decoding.
Rewrite: “I narrow the promise to what can be sold and enforced.”

The supporting copy is substantively useful. “At Postmates I was a product analyst in the deliver-anything era” could be more direct:
Rewrite: “At Postmates, I was a product analyst when the promise was to deliver anything.”
Keep the qualification that the analysis “made the case”; do not promote it into an unsupported claim that he personally changed the company’s strategy.

6. /full-time introduction: “I want one seat: the first person at an early-stage company who both builds the product and sells it.”
The intent is right, but “one seat: the first person” is a slightly awkward construction.
Rewrite: “I want one full-time role: an early-stage company’s first person to build the product and sell it.”

“I have thirteen years inside B2B software companies and $20M+ in revenue behind my work.”
“Inside B2B software companies” adds weight without meaning.
Rewrite: “I have thirteen years in B2B software and $20M+ in revenue behind my work.”

7. THE RECORD, Neuton sentence: “I helped launch Neuton.AI, with North American positioning for an AI product years before anyone was queuing to buy AI.”
“Anyone” is an unnecessary universal claim; the queue image sounds like retrospective self-promotion. The specific contribution is better.
Rewrite: “I helped launch Neuton.AI and worked on its North American positioning.”
Keep the subsequent technology-acquisition sentence separate, as it is now.

8. THE RECORD, remaining wording and layout:
“I worked inside four companies that reached an exit, with disclosed deals totaling $5B+, and I joined each one early.”
Three credentials are packed into one sentence.
Rewrite: “I worked inside four companies that reached an exit, joining each early. Disclosed deals total $5B+.”

“At SurveyMonkey, I contributed $1M+ in enterprise sales toward its 2018 IPO.”
“Toward its IPO” blurs the distinction between his contribution and the company event.
Rewrite: “At SurveyMonkey, I contributed $1M+ in enterprise sales. The company went public in 2018.”

“My name was on the cap table at SurveyMonkey and Guardicore.”
“My name was” is unnecessarily indirect.
Rewrite: “I was on the cap table at SurveyMonkey and Guardicore.”

At 390, this section becomes a long, uninterrupted block. Separate the opening summary from the company details so a reader can scan it.

9. CODE body: “It has active paying users in beta, and none has been lost to a competitor.”
The passive construction makes straightforward evidence sound like a carefully handled statistic.
Rewrite: “It is in beta with active paying users. None has left for a competitor.”
Keep that precise scope; it does not mean zero churn.

10. Principle-list design, especially 1440: The headings and rules extend far beyond the narrow evidence paragraphs. “SOLD.” and “HANDS.” become isolated final lines. That makes the declarations visually louder than their supporting work. Shorten and balance the headings, and bring the rules and heading measure into a more coherent relationship with the body column. At 390, preserve the readable body size and stacked list.

The type, texture, restrained rules and footer belong to the supplied /about design. Nothing visibly overflows horizontally. The partial lines beneath the sticky header in the scrolled captures are not, by themselves, evidence of a defect.

11. Missing from /full-time: a direct route from a claim to further evidence. The page asks the reader to accept the examples, then write. Reuse the existing “See the case studies →” link to /work beside the examples. Also put a quiet “Write to me” email link beneath the introduction so a reader already persuaded does not have to reach the bottom. Both additions use information already present on the site.
