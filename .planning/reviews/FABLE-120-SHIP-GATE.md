practitioner. Each page names a claim, gives the mechanism, and routes to one service. Nothing on
the six pages would make a careful buyer distrust the page. What each reader meets, and the lines
that carry or cost trust:

**/work.** The VP reads "$14M in revenue, sourced and closed, at a $1.2M average enterprise deal"
beside the Tel Aviv frame, then the mechanism in one sentence (research, story moved, sat in the

a distrust trigger: "An award-winning author ... / $3M in signed contracts across eleven awards"

call: "$3M in signed contracts, eleven of them." Not a fix; "eleven awards" is his confirmed
wording.

microsegmentation before the product was finished." The one sentence a security buyer discounts

of a deployment behind a global systemically important bank, the sentence before it supplies that
mechanism, and the ledger restores it on the operator's word ("do not re-cut it"). Keep. The FAQ
"Why would positioning change revenue?" answers the VP's first objection in two sentences. Routes

  activist with a platform. Nothing links the two studies: no cross-link sentence remains (the

  lines differ in kind (consultant vs activist), and ORDANI sits between them in the /work order
  and the Next chain, so they are never adjacent. The joint profile a reader would have to
  assemble on purpose (author, consultant, activist, government training, courses) is still not a
  name without outside knowledge.
- **Residual risk, outside the pages:** his public LinkedIn (linked in the footer) and its
  recommendations. The site cannot control that and should not try; the rule is simply never to
  add a detail that meets it halfway.

**Ruling: keep every descriptor as rendered.** No replacement strings. Standing rule for later
