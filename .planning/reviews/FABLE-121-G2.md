## 1. The popup: three directions in plain words, and my pick

**A. One voice.** /work starts speaking the language the home already speaks. A big uppercase
heading in the home's type, a plain description of what the page holds, then a small designed
entry into the Guardicore study: the photograph, the $14M with the hand-drawn circle around it,
and a "READ THIS ONE FIRST" line. Click it and the photograph flies into the study's opening
band. Below, the four other engagements as a designed ledger where the numbers are set large,
the method sentence sits as a quiet break, and the record of the four exits sits on the same
dark espresso ground as the home's receipts. Every study gets one to three drawn exhibits in the
body and the hand marks as anchors. No rule is touched. It is not plain, but three of the five
studies still open on words alone.

**B. Five worlds.** Everything in A, plus the home's colour worlds arrive on /work and the
studies. The heading and the featured entry sit on the home's terracotta ground; each of the
other four engagements gets its own full-width colour world with its number set at 120px, the
way the home sets $20M+. Each study opens on its own world colour instead of the shared dark
band, its section headings go uppercase, and its result gets a full-bleed figure section with
the circle drawn around it. Four things in B relax a written rule and are listed for your
yes or no: the study bands changing colour, the circle drawing itself in, a reveal on every
entry, and one exhibit zooming for longer than the site's motion cap. It is the loudest
option, and its cost is that /work becomes the home page told a second time.

**C. Five exhibits. Recommended.** Everything in A, with one structural change: every
engagement gets a drawing of how the work actually worked, in the same hand as the $20M+
circle, keyed to the real facts on its page. On /work, each entry shows its drawing at small
scale beside the number. In the study, the drawing takes the place a photograph would have
held in the opening band (Guardicore and ORDANI keep their photographs and get their drawing
as the first break in the body). Click any entry and its drawing flies into the study. That
fixes the three things you named at once: the studies with no pictures get a picture that
could not exist on any other site, a reader who will not read gets the story from the
drawings and the numbers alone, and the click is a designed object that moves. One optional
extra for your yes or no: the drawings drawing themselves in once as you reach them.

**Why C.** You asked for visual punch, animation, design skill on show, and a site that does
not look built by AI. A template can do colour fields and big type (B). A template cannot draw
the east-west traffic Guardicore's buyers could not see, or the six steps an RFP takes through
the engine with the marked gap in copper. Those drawings are the work itself, they are the
part no AI-built site has (audit A found three of your five studies carry zero images or
exhibits top to bottom), and they give the doorway motion something to carry on every entry,
not only the one with a photograph. C costs design time, not rules: five authored drawings,
each checked against the ledger before it ships. If the drawings are not ready at the ship
gate, C ships as A and the drawings land one study at a time.
