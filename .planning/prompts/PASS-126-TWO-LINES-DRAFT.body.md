You are drafting for micahjonesconsulting.com, the site of Micah Jones, a solo operator who builds products and sells

## What three independent reviewers said
- "Something named ships in month one." (flagged by all three) reads as a placeholder: "named" has no clear sense, and
  "named ships" parses first as a name ("a ship named...") until "in month one" lands.
- "You get a roadmap, and you sign off on it before I build anything." ends on "before I build", the same phrase the
  Plan headline just ended on, so the two lines stutter.
- "That means sign-in, data, deployment, and where it stands on compliance, written down." mixes three deliverables with
  a status description, and "written down" attaches to nothing clear.

## The facts each rewrite must keep, and must not go past
- PLAN: before the build starts, the client gets a roadmap and signs off on it. Within the first month, something
  specific (a defined, nameable piece of the work) ships. The month-one promise is ONLY that a defined piece ships; it is
  NEVER the full product, a production launch, or "the build" (earlier drafts inflated it that way; that is forbidden).
- BUILD: the build is production-grade, not a prototype: it includes sign-in (authentication), data, and deployment, and
  the client gets a written account of where the product stands on compliance. Do not claim the product IS compliant or
  certified; only that its compliance position is written down.
- Keep first person ("I", "you"), no "we". ASCII only (straight quotes, no dashes of any kind between words).
- None of the banned words listed at the end of this prompt, in any inflection.
