headings ("Operating principles"), the same preview-card row repeated ("Name / role / metric →"), a

The proposal: new hero "It works. / It just does not sell."; the two doors moved directly under the hero,
the right one rewritten; How I work = one larger paragraph with the single Guardicore proof and a "See the

fixed by a verified ledger and not yours to change. Wording may be critiqued.
