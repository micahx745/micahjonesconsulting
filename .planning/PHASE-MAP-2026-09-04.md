## 1. The rulings

Each has its reason. Each is reversible with one word from you. None gets re-litigated by
a later session without that word.

**A. The playbook page is the proof, not a product page.**
"landing page" is the single largest term in the asking corpus (27 distinct authors) and
you sell nothing against it. The one landing page a buyer can inspect is yours. So this
page serves two readers at once: the person buying the book, and the founder deciding
whether you could do this for them. Every choice on it is judged against both.

**B. Lead with the 9% pain. Keep the H1.**
*"The AI handed you the code. Now ship the company."* stays. It is a thesis, it is yours,
it is already distribution-forward (company, not code), and it passes R7. What moves: the
dek opens on *"It shipped. Nobody came."* (8.9% of r/buildinpublic bodies); the "If this is
you" block reorders to crickets → production → the wall, with the wall reworded to the
attested beat *"kept running into"* (13.4× lift, the highest in the dataset) and the
1.0%-measured regression line cut; title and meta carry "shipped, nobody came" and "vibe
coding" (attested by 10, the umbrella term). Reason: attested language goes where search
reads it and where a reader recognises himself. A Reddit complaint as an H1 reads as a
tell; a thesis reads as an author. The leg's alternatives A and C are on file if you
disagree.

**C. The book changes in three places, none of them its argument.**
All in `Code/the-80-percent-wall`, never the frozen copy here.
1. Free sample moves from chapter 1 (the 1.0% beat) to chapter 8, "The first ten users"
   (the dominant pain), **if** chapter 8 stands alone as a first read. Opus is checking now.
2. Chapter 10 ships an artifact. Every other chapter ends in a file; three reviewers
   unanimously said the last one ends in prose. A handoff checklist fixes the structural
   break without a rewrite.
3. Chapter 1's opening beat is re-pitched to the attested wording. The argument (the wall is
   arithmetic) is untouched; the sentence that carries it changes.
Not a marketing book. The leg's guardrail stands: chapters 8–9 are about 13 of 69 pages, so
the promise is *"ship it and get your first ten users"*, which the book already delivers.
Price stays $99 launch / $149 after.

**D. The offer the data found gets built, after the page.**
A fixed-price "landing page + first ten users" package for AI-built apps. It ships in Pass
99, not 98, because the reworked playbook page is its portfolio. My recommendation is the
leg's: repurpose the $500 tier rather than add a fourth SKU, because three prices is a
choice and four is a menu. But you named and shaped the Unstick Session. Parked, §3.

**E. Spectacular means evidence, inside the constitution.**
The page is graded against DESIGN_BAR R1–R20, and the load-bearing five (R1, R4, R6, R12,
R20) are not negotiable. So: one accent (the Color Worlds palette, no new colour); the
WallChart stays the one figure and there is no second animation; R12 imagery is the book's
own material, the nine line-drawn diagrams, the pre-flight cards, the SPEC files, rendered
as real artifacts, not decoration. Where the investment goes: type scale and vertical
rhythm; showing more of the actual book; a primary buy action with the refund beside it;
and the second credential. Those last two are Pass-61's three unresolved purchase blockers
(no primary buy action, refund far from the button, nothing answers "who are you"). What it
is not: a template redesign, gradients, new motion, a different page.

**F. Nothing sells over a dead webhook.**
The book's checkout is built and unwired. It gets wired in Pass 98 and the button ships
**only after** the live Stripe destination is registered and one live buy-and-refund has
run (`docs/MONEY-RAIL-TEST.md`). That is yours, §3. The launch date can be set at the ship
gate; the page does not wait on it.

**G. The site follows the page.**
Home already says *"I build the go-to-market."* It stays as is until the playbook page has
shipped and been judged. Then home, `/services` and `/packages` get the distribution
reframe in Passes 99–100. One proof before a site-wide pivot; the leg's own confidence on
the home spear is Medium.
